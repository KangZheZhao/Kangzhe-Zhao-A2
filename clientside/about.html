The organisation

From a morning run to a seat at the table.

Sydney Community Giving brings neighbours together around practical local causes through welcoming events.

The calendar includes runs, markets, music, art and dinners. Each event page shows what the activity supports, where and when it happens, the ticket donation, and progress towards its fundraising goal.

Where funds go

What these events support.

  • Food relief and practical care packs
  • Youth mentoring and creative learning
  • Community housing support
  • Accessible wellbeing activities and meals
